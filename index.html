Design meets production

The process behind every logo rebuild — applied everywhere else too.

  1. Assess

    Start by evaluating what exists — a low-res logo, an old brand mark, a rough brief — and identify exactly what needs rebuilding or clarifying.

  2. Rebuild

    Redraw or design in vector — Illustrator, CorelDraw — so the output scales cleanly from a business card to a building directory.

  3. Render

    For packaging and product work, build out 3D mockups and product shots in Maya, SketchUp, and KeyShot before anything goes to print.

  4. Deliver

    Hand off production-ready files with the technical accuracy of a shop drawing — checked against real materials and print methods, not just screen color.

About

I'm Sherwin Bonavente, a graphic designer based in Pasig City, currently Graphic Design Manager at Kite and Crest, where I produce shirt designs across a range of styles and materials with brand identity and audience in mind.

My background sits a little outside the usual graphic design lane — alongside branding, packaging, and print, I work in 3D modeling and rendering (Autodesk Maya, SketchUp, KeyShot) and technical shop drawing. That combination means I don't just hand off a pretty file — I think about how a design actually holds up once it's printed, sewn, or rendered as a real product.

TESDA NC3-certified in Visual Graphic Design, with Adobe Photoshop and Illustrator Expert certifications, and a Lean Six Sigma White Belt for the process-and-quality side of the work.
